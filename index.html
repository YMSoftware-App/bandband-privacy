Privacy Policy for BandBand

Last updated: September 7, 2026

BandBand ("we", "our", "the app") is a mobile app for band event management — organizing gigs and rehearsals, tracking participants and payments, managing song lists, and communicating within a band. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Account information — name, email address, and phone number (optional), collected when you sign up via Google, Apple, LINE, or email and password.
  • Profile details — profile photo, instruments you play, a short bio, and your city, all of which you enter voluntarily to help other musicians find and recognize you.
  • Approximate location — with your permission, used only to suggest your city and to help you find nearby musicians and bands. This is a one-time "while using the app" lookup, not continuous location tracking.
  • Content you create or upload — band photos, event flyers, music sheet submissions, venue photos, and messages you post in an event's Q&A thread.
  • Event and participation records — which bands and events you've joined, your role (e.g. singer, guest, crew), song sign-ups, and payment status that a band admin records for that event (BandBand does not process payments itself — admins track who has paid in cash or by other means outside the app; no card or bank details are collected by BandBand).
  • Device push token — a device identifier used solely to deliver push notifications (e.g. new events, payment reminders, Q&A activity) to your device.

How We Use Information

  • To operate the app's core features: bands, events, participants, song lists, music sheet review, Q&A, and venue directory.
  • To send push notifications you've opted into, respecting your per-event and global notification preferences.
  • To suggest your city and show nearby musicians/bands/venues.
  • To let band admins manage event logistics (who's coming, who's paid, song order) within the bands you belong to.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing With Third-Party Services

BandBand is built on the following third-party infrastructure, each of which processes data only as needed to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, file storage, and authentication.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Google, Apple, and LINE — provide sign-in when you choose those options; we only receive your basic profile (name, email) from them.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — used to search and verify venue addresses; address queries you type are sent to this service.
  • Google Maps — used for map links and navigation when you tap "Navigate" or "Google Maps" on a venue.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes your profile and personal data from our systems. Content tied to a band or event you participated in (e.g. a Q&A message, a song sign-up) may be retained in de-identified or band-owned form where it's part of another user's or band's shared history.

Children's Privacy

BandBand is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

  • Location permission can be denied or revoked at any time in your device settings; city-based features will simply be unavailable.
  • Push notifications can be disabled globally or per-event inside the app, or turned off entirely in your device settings.
  • You can review and edit your profile information at any time in the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to ymliao@beingfreebutterfact.com.
