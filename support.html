BandBand Support

Last updated: October 25, 2026

BandBand is a mobile app for band event management — organizing gigs and rehearsals, tracking participants and payments, managing song lists, sheet music, venues, and communicating within a band. If something isn't working, or you have a question, this page tells you how to reach us.

Contact Us

For bug reports, feature requests, or any other question about the app, email ym.liao@gmail.com. We read every message and aim to reply within a few days.

Common Questions

  • I can't sign in. BandBand supports Google, Apple, LINE, and email + password sign-in. Make sure you're using the same method you originally signed up with — if you're not sure which one, email us and we can check.
  • How do I delete my account? Open the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile and personal data.
  • How do I change my language? BandBand is available in English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, and Korean. Switch it anytime from the welcome screen's language selector, or from Settings → Language once signed in.
  • How do I report an issue with payments? BandBand doesn't process payments itself — a band's admin tracks who has paid outside the app. If something looks wrong in the app's display of payment status, email us with the band/event name and we'll look into it.
  • I found a bug. Email us a short description of what you did and what happened — a screenshot helps a lot if you have one.
